## How to use this worksheet

Write your answers here first, then copy them into Pages CMS one form at a time. The headings below match the eight forms in Pages CMS, in the same order, and each question uses the form's own label.

1. Make your own copy of this worksheet (or print it) and fill it in. Work in plain text; you can polish later.
2. Gather your images and your résumé PDF in one folder. Name each file plainly, with words that say what it is: `lantern-level-2.jpg`, not `IMG_4471 (1).JPG`.
3. Open Pages CMS and copy your answers in, form by form. Save after each form.

Some hints apply everywhere:

- **Empty means hidden.** Any section you leave blank (Gallery, Experience, Education, Tools, About, LinkedIn, GitHub) simply doesn't appear on your site. Leave a section empty rather than fill it with something weak.
- **Order** is a number: 1 appears first, then 2, and so on.
- **Alt text** is one sentence saying what an image shows. It's read aloud to people who can't see the image, and search engines use it. Write it for every image.
- **Addresses:** type full web addresses, starting `https://`.
- *Optional* means you can skip it.

## Images and files you'll need

You upload one well-sized original of each image; the site makes smaller copies for phones by itself. JPG suits screenshots and artwork, and PNG suits pixel art or images with sharp text.

## 2. Selected Work

One entry per piece of work. Copy the block below once for each entry; three to five strong entries beat ten thin ones.

Each entry has a **Kind**, shown as a small label before its title on your home page and above the title on its own page. You can change it later without losing anything:

- **Case study:** your fullest work, told as a story in Sections. A full-width card at the top of Selected Work and a long page. One or two at most.
- **Project:** a standard card and page, with Sections if you like.
- **Brief:** one line in a list on the home page and a short page. No Sections. Good for jams and small pieces.
